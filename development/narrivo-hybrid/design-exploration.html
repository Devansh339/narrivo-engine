NARRIVO ARTISTIC DIRECTION

Character Engine Visual Design Exploration

Comparative aesthetic study across three distinct visual paradigms using the exact same character concept: "The Aviator / Field Commander".

Character Concept: Intrepid Explorer / Aviator • Key Elements: Leather shearling jacket, high brass collar, utility holster, rugged field boots, sculpted jawline, styled parted hair
Direction 1
Premium 2D Illustrated
Feature animation & graphic novel aesthetic. Dynamic variable-weight ink, bold stylized angular contours, cross-contour hatching, and graphic cel shadows.
  • ✓ Inking: Calligraphic brush outlines with variable line weight tapering at joints.
  • ✓ Shading: Crisp 2-tone graphic cel cuts with subtle screen-tone hatching accents.
  • ✓ Anatomy: Sharp, stylized facial planes with sculpted graphic novel nose and jaw.
  • ✓ Best For: High-energy storytime narratives and bold expressive caricatures.
Direction 2
Premium Stylized 3D
Modern feature film aesthetic (Pixar/Overwatch). Zero black outlines, soft ambient occlusion in deep creases, warm subsurface skin scatter, and polished specular highlights.
  • ✓ Inking: Outline-free form defined purely by smooth lighting and tone gradients.
  • ✓ Shading: Smooth cylindrical shading, subsurface scatter orange falloff, leather specular sheen.
  • ✓ Anatomy: Sculpted, volumetric curves with soft cheek rounding and sculpted lips.
  • ✓ Best For: High-budget cinematic shorts with realistic shallow-depth lighting.
Direction 3
Hybrid Dimensional
The Narrivo sweet spot: Crisp illustrated vector linework infused with directional volumetric gradients, scene rim light glints, and ambient occlusion depth.
  • ✓ Inking: Subtle tinted vector lines with softened ink that reacts to local lighting.
  • ✓ Shading: Directional warm key light + cool ambient fill + glowing rim light edge.
  • ✓ Integration: Blends seamlessly into 3D Three.js sets while keeping 2D expressive freedom.
  • ✓ Best For: Narrivo Hybrid flagship productions (the optimal visual identity).

Artistic Breakdown of Stage 1 & 2 Weaknesses

1. Uniform Vector Line Weight

Stage 1 used a uniform 3.5px line across the entire body, creating a dated "Flash/ClipArt" impression. Natural animation artwork requires line modulation (thick in occlusions, thin on highlights).

2. Flat Cel Blocks

Every surface used a single unmodulated fill plus a flat shadow rectangle, ignoring form curvature, subsurface skin warming, and secondary ambient bounce.

3. Boxy Torso & Pelvis

The torso was constructed as a single trapezoid with minimal hip articulation, hiding natural athletic shoulder-to-waist taper and clothing drape tension.

4. Geometric Facial Planes

Facial elements were based on simple ellipses and arcs without sculpted philtrum, eyelid crease depth, or stylized jaw corner definition.
